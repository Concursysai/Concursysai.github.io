Mavis

A personal voice assistant that runs on one person's own computer.

Mavis is a private, self-hosted assistant built by Joel Mead for his own use. It is not a public service: there are no accounts to sign up for, and it is not offered to anyone else.

With its owner's permission it can read his Gmail to sort new mail and save draft replies. It never sends an email without his explicit approval, and his mail stays on his own computer.
