Privacy policy

Mavis · last updated 3 November 2026

Who uses Mavis

Mavis is a personal assistant that runs on its owner's own computer. It is used only by its owner, Joel Mead. It is not offered to the public, and nobody else can sign in to it.

What Google data it uses

  • Gmail, read-only (gmail.readonly): to sort new mail into categories such as urgent, reply needed and low priority, and to summarise it for its owner.
  • Gmail drafts (gmail.compose): to save draft replies when its owner asks. A draft is sent only after its owner explicitly approves that specific email.

Where the data goes

  • Mail is processed on the owner's own computer. Email bodies are not stored. For up to 7 days Mavis keeps the sender, subject, a category and a one-line summary, on that computer only.
  • To sort and summarise mail, the text of new messages is sent to the AI model the owner uses (Anthropic's Claude) under the owner's own account. It is not used for advertising.
  • Google user data is never sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.
  • Mavis's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can revoke Mavis's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which stops all access. Deleting Mavis's local sign-in file and its 7-day summaries removes everything it kept.
